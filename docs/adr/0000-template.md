## Context

doing nothing unacceptable. Written so that a reader who was not in the room understands
the pressure, not just the outcome.

## Decision

What we do, concretely enough to check the code against it. Name modules, tables,
mechanisms. Avoid adjectives.

## Consequences

## How to invalidate

decision was not a decision.
